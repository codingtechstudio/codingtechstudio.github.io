How we work

A small team with a simple promise: games that respect the player's time, money and privacy.

01

Fun first

Every game starts as a small prototype. If the core idea is not fun in the first minute, we do not build the rest.

02

Fair play

Free to play means free to finish. No fake timers, no hidden costs, no ads dressed up as content.

03

Privacy by default

We ask before we measure, collect as little as we can and publish a clear privacy policy for every title.

04

Made for everyone

Our store pages are written in ten languages, and our games are built to be played with one hand, in short breaks.
